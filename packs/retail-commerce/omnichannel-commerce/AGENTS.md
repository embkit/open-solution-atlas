# AGENTS.md — Omnichannel Commerce (L1)

L1 brief. At L2: order state machine and inventory deduction semantics are
the load-bearing decisions (overselling is the cardinal sin); every money
operation needs idempotency keys and reconciliation specs; PCI scope must be
demonstrated out of the core (PSP-hosted fields); promotions evaluated on a
pricing snapshot, never recomputed after order capture; mini-program/POS are
channel variants.
